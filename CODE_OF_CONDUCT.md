# Code of conduct

## Our standard

VolForge welcomes respectful, technically rigorous collaboration. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- make room for different experience levels and perspectives;
- give and accept specific, constructive review feedback;
- disclose numerical limitations, data assumptions, and conflicts of interest; and
- protect private, proprietary, and security-sensitive information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, code review, project discussions, and any
public setting where someone is representing VolForge.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Security vulnerabilities must use
the process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports in confidence, may ask
for additional context, and may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions or restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity,
pattern, and impact; retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
